# Consolidated Risk Scoring & Prioritized Investigation Queue Report

This report documents the design, scoring formula, weights, validation results, and top prioritized findings for the **Consolidated Risk Scoring, Investigation Queue, and Provider Risk Stage**.

---

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this stage is to unify the outputs of three independent, downstream anomaly detection channels:
1.  **Rule-Based Anomaly Detection** (Deduplicated explicit business rule violations)
2.  **Statistical Outlier Analysis** (IQR-based numerical feature shifts)
3.  **Machine Learning Isolation Forest Models** (Unsupervised multi-dimensional feature-space anomalies)

By combining these disparate signals, the system generates a single, transparent, and explainable **overall risk score (0-100)** for every record. This allows investigators to prioritize claims based on cumulative joint risk evidence rather than raw flag counts.

## 3. Detection Signals Used
The scoring engine consumes four primary signal types:
1.  **Rule-Based violations**: Explicit business constraints (e.g. Paid > Allowed, Processed < Submission).
2.  **Statistical outliers**: Deviances from the baseline training distribution ($Z$-score magnitudes).
3.  **Unsupervised ML anomalies**: Multi-feature isolation path lengths (Isolation Forest percentile).
4.  **Consensus Agreement**: Coincidence of flags across the three detection channels.

---

## 4. Risk Scoring Formula & Weighting Methodology
The overall risk score is computed on a 0-100 scale using the following weighted components:

$$\text{Overall\_Risk\_Score} = 0.40 \times \text{rule\_risk\_score} + 0.25 \times \text{statistical\_risk\_score} + 0.25 \times \text{ml\_risk\_score} + 0.10 \times \text{consensus\_score}$$

### Component Normalized Scores (0-100)
1.  **`rule_risk_score`** (Weight: 40%):
    *   Map rule violations to points: Low severity = 2 points, Medium severity = 5 points, High severity = 10 points.
    *   $$\text{Rule\_Risk\_Score} = \min\left(100.0, 100.0 \times \frac{\text{sum\_of\_points}}{20.0}\right)$$
    *   *Rationale*: Prevents artificial inflation from multiple minor rule violations by capping the normalized score at 100 (points capped at 20).
2.  **`statistical_risk_score`** (Weight: 25%):
    *   Compare continuous variables against training split statistics to calculate $Z$-score deviations ($Z = |x - \mu|/\sigma$):
        *   If $1.5 < Z \le 3.0$: 3 points (moderate deviation)
        *   If $Z > 3.0$: 6 points (large deviation)
    *   $$\text{Statistical\_Risk\_Score} = \min\left(100.0, 100.0 \times \frac{\text{sum\_of\_points}}{12.0}\right)$$
    *   *Rationale*: Incorporates the magnitude of deviance, prioritizing extreme numerical outliers.
3.  **`ml_risk_score`** (Weight: 25%):
    *   Equal to the `Anomaly_Percentile` (0 to 100) generated by the Isolation Forest model.
    *   *Rationale*: Provides a smooth, continuous indicator of multi-dimensional feature space isolation.
4.  **`consensus_score`** (Weight: 10%):
    *   Consensus Level 0 (no flags) = 0.0
    *   Consensus Level 1 (one flag) = 20.0
    *   Consensus Level 2 (two flags) = 60.0
    *   Consensus Level 3 (three flags) = 100.0
    *   *Rationale*: Directly rewards agreement between independent detection channels.

## 5. Risk Category Thresholds & Priorities
Four score brackets define risk categories and Recommended Priorities:
*   **LOW** (Score 0 to 24.99) -> **P4 - Low** priority.
*   **MEDIUM** (Score 25 to 49.99) -> **P3 - Medium** priority.
*   **HIGH** (Score 50 to 74.99) -> **P2 - High** priority.
*   **CRITICAL** (Score 75 to 100) -> **P1 - Critical** priority.

---

## 6. Investigation Queue Methodology & Sorting
The investigation queue is saved in `investigation_queue.csv` and sorted hierarchically:
1.  `recommended_priority` ascending (`P1` > `P2` > `P3` > `P4`).
2.  `overall_risk_score` descending.
3.  `highest_rule_severity` descending (`High` > `Medium` > `Low` > `None`).
4.  `consensus_level` descending.

This guarantees that the most suspicious records with joint detection evidence appear at the very top.

---

## 7. Provider-Level Risk Aggregation Methodology
Records are grouped by `Provider_NPI`. Claims with missing NPIs are explicitly grouped under `"UNKNOWN_PROVIDER"`.
*   Providers are flagged as `"PROVIDER REQUIRING REVIEW"` (Status) and `"HIGH-RISK PROVIDER PATTERN"` (Risk Level) if:
    *   Any submitted claim has a `CRITICAL` or `HIGH` risk level.
    *   Their flagged claim percentage exceeds 10.0%.
    *   Their average claim risk score exceeds 35.0.
